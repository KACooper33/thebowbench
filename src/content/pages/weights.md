## The ring test applies to the bow, not to the weight

The [under $500 riser comparison](/risers/under-500/) on this site leaves its 12.2 cm ring column blank, because the ring test in the [2022 World Archery bylaw](#source-wa-dampener-bylaw) applies to the whole bow "including any combination of weights, vibration dampeners, and other attachments", not to a riser on its own. The same limit applies here in reverse: no maker can honestly certify that a weight passes the ring, because that depends on the riser and everything else fitted alongside it.

One product on this page is the exception, and it is worth reading precisely. Gillo publishes a specific claim for the G4 Hammers Weight Kit: it "can pass inside the 12.2 cm BB ring." That claim covers the kit fitted to the riser models Gillo lists it for, GQ, GT, G2 and Ghost. It is not a guarantee for a complete bow carrying other attachments too. The whole assembly still needs checking before an event, on this riser or any other.

## Universal thread against a riser-specific mount

Two of the three weights on this page use a 5/16-24 thread, the same stabiliser bushing thread already published on several risers covered elsewhere on this site, including the [WNS Quantum AX](/risers/under-500/) and the Kinetic Vygo. A weight on that thread fits any riser with a standard bushing.

The Gillo kit does not. It mounts to specific Gillo risers only, through a centre M6 screw rather than a universal thread. That is the trade a buyer makes for the published ring claim: fit is narrower, and the maker states more about what the result does.

## What the numbers show

**Mass.** Mybo MYKAN C is 100 g. Yost Mojo Weight is 454 g, over four times as much in one piece. Gillo G4 Hammers Kit is 600 g across the whole kit, two hammers plus hardware.

**Price.** Mybo $19.99, Yost $56.30, Gillo $264.99. The Gillo kit costs more than four times the Yost weight for less than a third more mass, which is the fit and the ring claim, not raw weight, that the price is paying for.

**Material.** All three are stainless steel.

## Which one should you buy?

Buy the Yost Archery Mojo Weight if you want one weight that does most of the tuning work on a standard riser bushing. At 454 g it is heavy enough to matter and fits any riser with a 5/16-24 thread.

Buy the Mybo MYKAN C if you are adding small, incremental weight to a setup that is already close to right, or want to start light and add more.

Buy the Gillo G4 Hammers Kit only if you already own a compatible Gillo riser. Its price and its published ring claim are both tied to that specific fit, and neither carries over to a different maker's riser.

Whatever you buy, check the complete bow against the 12.2 cm ring yourself before an event. A maker's claim, where one exists, covers its own product on the risers it names, not your finished setup.

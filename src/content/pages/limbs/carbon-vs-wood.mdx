## "Carbon vs wood" is really carbon-and-foam against wood-and-fibreglass

Neither limb on this page is built from a single material, and the label on a listing usually names only the facing, not the whole limb.

The **Galaxy Bronze Star** is fibreglass over a hard maple wood core. The **WNS Motive C5** is carbon fibre over a foam core. Calling them "wood" and "carbon" is the shorthand the trade uses, and this page keeps it because that is the term readers search for, but the real difference is core material as much as facing.

Most limbs marketed as "carbon" at this price point are carbon-foam, not carbon-wood. A small number of far more expensive limbs use a carbon-wood hybrid core, which is a third category and not covered on this page.

## What the numbers actually show

**Price.** The Galaxy Bronze Star is $99.99. The WNS Motive C5 is $149.99, $50 more.

**Draw weight.** The Bronze Star is published from 16 to 40 lb, in 2 lb steps. The Motive C5 is published from 20 to 46 lb, in 2 lb steps, extending 6 lb higher at the top.

**Length.** The Bronze Star publishes three lengths: short, medium and long. The Motive C5's Lancaster listing does not publish a length option at all. If length matters to your draw, confirm it with the maker before buying.

**Fitting.** Both are ILF. The Bronze Star's listing adds that it also fits Hoyt Grand Prix risers.

No maker on this page publishes limb mass weight, draw force curve or arrow speed. A gear guide that compares carbon and wood limbs on "faster" or "smoother" without a published or measured number is repeating a claim, not reporting one.

## What draw weight actually means for barebow

Barebow has no draw weight limit under [World Archery Book 3](#source-wa-book-3). The 60 lb figure that shows up on gear sites belongs to the compound division, under article 9.2.1, not to barebow.

That matters here because both limbs on this page publish ranges well under that figure, and neither publisher's range is a barebow-specific restriction. It is simply what each product is built for.

## Which one should you buy?

Buy the Galaxy Bronze Star if this is your first set of limbs. Wood-core limbs are the standard starting point in barebow, they cost less, and $99.99 leaves more of a beginner's budget for a riser and a plunger.

Buy the WNS Motive C5 once you know your draw weight and have outgrown an entry-level limb. Carbon-foam limbs are lighter and faster to recover after the shot, which is a real published material difference, not a marketing claim.

Do not buy on draw weight range alone. A 6 lb difference at the top of a published range rarely decides a purchase on its own; it matters only if your intended poundage sits in that gap.

## Where these numbers came from

Every specification and price is as published by [Lancaster Archery Supply](#source-lancaster-limbs), read on 13 August 2026. Draw weight and length came from each product's own variant selector on the listing, not from marketing copy, so they reflect what a buyer actually chooses at checkout.

Prices are one retailer's, on one day, in one country. Neither limb on this page has been handled, weighed or shot by this site.

Limbs set your draw weight, and draw weight in barebow is less regulated than most archers expect. World Archery Book 3 sets no draw weight limit for the barebow division. The 60 lb figure that circulates widely is article 9.2.1, and it applies to the compound division, not barebow.

Length and limb fitting have to match the riser, almost always ILF on a modern barebow setup, and length is usually chosen as a pair with riser length to reach a workable total bow length for your draw.

Material is the other real choice: carbon against a wood core changes weight, feel and price. See the [carbon vs wood comparison](/limbs/carbon-vs-wood/) for the two set side by side.

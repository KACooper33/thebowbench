The tab decision that actually changes which product you need is not material or face plate. It is whether you string walk.

World Archery permits marks on the tab face for barebow, "uniform in size, shape and colour", with up to two different lengths under article 9.3.7 of Book 3. A stringwalking tab uses those marks as crawl references. If you shoot a fixed anchor with a gap instead, the marks are wasted, and a plainer tab does the same job for less.

Traditional archery bans both string walking and tab marks outright, so a barebow tab bought for its crawl marks is not legal traditional equipment. The [barebow vs traditional guide](/guides/barebow-vs-traditional/) covers the full split between the two divisions.

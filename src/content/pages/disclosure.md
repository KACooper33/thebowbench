## The Bow Bench earns nothing

This site has no affiliate programme, no advertising, and no sponsorship. Nobody pays The Bow Bench anything.

There is no commission on any link here. A link to a shop is a link to a shop, and clicking it pays this site nothing.

That is the position today, and this page says so plainly rather than describing money the site does not make.

## What has to happen before that changes

Two things, in this order.

1. **An affiliate programme accepts the site.** The networks that carry archery retailers review applicants and want a live site with real traffic. None has been applied to.
2. **This page is rewritten first, and the disclosure returns to the top of every page carrying a paid link.** United States law requires it, and it goes up before the first paid link, not after.

Until both have happened, take every link on this site as earning nothing.

## What will not change when the site does earn

A commission will never buy a recommendation. No manufacturer or retailer pays to appear on this site, and none is given a preview of a page before it publishes.

Where a product is a poor choice, the page says so, including when that product would pay more than the one it loses to. Every comparison page names one overall pick, one budget pick and one premium pick, and those picks are made before any paid link is added.

The gear makers written about most often on this site have no affiliate programmes at all. That means the site would earn nothing from many of the products it recommends most highly, whatever happens later.

## How the numbers on this site are produced

Every number on a comparison page is marked as one of three things.

**Measured** means the number was taken on our own bench, with the method described on the page. The page states what was weighed, what tool was used, and what was not tested.

**Maker** means the number was published by the manufacturer and has not been independently checked.

**Retailer** means a shop stated it on the record, in a listing, a policy page or an answer published under its own name. A price is a retailer figure, and so is a warranty term or a returns window. It is neither our measurement nor the maker's claim, and calling it either would be inaccurate.

Most numbers on this site are maker or retailer figures. One bow has been measured here so far, and the pages say which.

No number on this site is estimated, inferred or copied from another site. Where a figure is not known, the table shows a dash rather than a guess.

## Why this page exists

United States law requires a clear and conspicuous disclosure of a material connection between a publisher and the products it recommends. An affiliate commission is such a connection.

There is no such connection today, so there is nothing to disclose. This page exists anyway, for two reasons. A reader deserves a straight answer to "how does this site make money", and the answer is currently "it does not". And when that changes, the change should be visible on a page that already existed rather than appearing at the same moment as the first paid link.

A short disclosure will return to the top of every page carrying a paid link at that point. It is not there now, because it would be describing something that is not happening.

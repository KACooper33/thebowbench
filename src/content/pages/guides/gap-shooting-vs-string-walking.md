## What the two methods are

**Gap shooting** aims by using the arrow point as the reference, and holding it a judged distance above or below the target depending on range. The anchor on the face stays in one place. The gap changes.

**String walking** aims by moving the drawing fingers up or down the string, below the nocking point, which changes the angle of the arrow without moving the point off the target. The anchor stays in one place, and the position on the string changes. The distance the fingers move down is called the crawl.

Both are aiming methods, not equipment classes. This page covers what each one requires from your gear and which divisions permit it. How to actually shoot either one is coaching, and belongs with a coach.

## Both are legal in barebow

[World Archery Book 3 article 9.3.5](#source-wa-book-3) is four words long on this point: "Face and string walking are permitted."

So the choice inside barebow is yours. Neither method carries a rules penalty, and you are not choosing between a legal option and a tolerated one.

## Only one of them needs equipment

This is the practical difference, and it is the reason this page sits under tabs rather than under technique.

**Gap shooting needs nothing special.** Any tab that protects your fingers will do. Nothing about the method requires a mark, a plate or a particular shape.

**String walking needs a tab you can measure against.** The crawl has to be repeatable, which means the tab needs marks, and you need to be able to find the same one each time.

The rules cap what those marks may be. [Article 9.3.7](#source-wa-book-3) permits "marks or lines added directly to the tab or on tape placed on the face of the tab", provided they "must be uniform in size, shape and colour and may have up to two different lengths". A tab with a graduated ruler printed on it, or marks in several sizes and colours, is not legal barebow equipment.

Not every tab sold for barebow publishes crawl marks. Of three tabs checked here, two state the feature outright, and the third, despite being a well-known barebow name, does not claim it on its listing at all. That matters a great deal if string walking is why you are buying, so check the listing rather than the category it sits in.

## The choice decides which divisions you can enter

This is the part that is easy to miss, and it is worth knowing before you spend a season learning one method.

String walking is permitted in barebow. It is banned in the two divisions closest to barebow:

- **World Archery traditional**, [article 19.4.5](#source-wa-book-4): "A single anchor or face walking is permitted. String walking is not permitted."
- **World Archery longbow**, [article 19.5.4](#source-wa-book-4): "string and face walking are not permitted."
- **NFAA Traditional**, which states that ["One anchor point only is permitted."](#source-nfaa-styles)

Gap shooting, which uses one anchor and no crawl, is compatible with all of those.

So a gap shooter can move between barebow, traditional and longbow without changing how they aim. A string walker who wants to shoot traditional has to learn a second method first. Neither is a reason to pick one over the other, but it is a reason to know which you are choosing.

The [barebow vs traditional guide](/guides/barebow-vs-traditional/) sets out the full equipment differences between those divisions.

## What this page will not tell you

Which method will make you shoot better.

That depends on your eyes, your draw length, the distances you shoot, and what your coach sees when you draw. It is the same reason the [poundage guide](/guides/what-poundage-for-barebow/) publishes no draw weight.

What this site can tell you is what each method costs in equipment and what it permits in competition. Both of those are written down in the rulebooks, and both are above.

## Before you buy a tab

If you have not decided yet, buy a tab with marks. It costs little more, gap shooting works perfectly well with it, and it leaves string walking open without a second purchase.

If you have decided on gap shooting and expect to shoot traditional or longbow, note that the traditional division bans tab markings outright under [article 19.4.7](#source-wa-book-4), so the tab you buy for barebow may not follow you across.

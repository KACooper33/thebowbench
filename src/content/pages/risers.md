Mass weight is the number most gear pages lead with, and it is the least useful one on its own. A barebow archer adds weights below and above the grip to reach the balance that suits them, so the riser's own weight is only a starting point.

The number that matters more is the weight system: where a riser lets you mount weights, and whether it ships with any. Two risers at the same published mass can end up nowhere near each other once weights are fitted.

The [2022 World Archery bylaw](/guides/barebow-rules/) permits weights and dampeners below and above the grip, provided the complete bow still passes the 12.2 cm ring. No maker publishes a ring result for a bare riser, because the ring applies to the whole bow with its attachments fitted, not to the riser alone.
